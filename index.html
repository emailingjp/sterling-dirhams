Each figure is the difference between dealing now and dealing at that moment, after the dealing charge. Green means now brings more dirhams, red means fewer. Click a figure to chart against it. The market trades around the clock from 01:00 Monday to 01:00 Saturday Dubai, so these are two fixed moments in the London day, not an opening and a close: 8am is when London starts dealing, and 4pm is when the benchmark rate that UK banks use is set. Trading carries on after both, in New York and then Asia.

How news usually moves your rate

The dirham is pinned to the dollar, so your rate is GBP/USD in disguise. Anything that strengthens the dollar against the pound gives you fewer dirhams, and anything that strengthens the pound gives you more.

If this happensBecauseUsual effectYour dirhams
US inflation or jobs figures stronger than forecastFed rises become more likely and US yields riseDollar strongerFewer
US inflation or jobs figures weaker than forecastFed rises become less likely and US yields fallDollar weakerMore
The Fed raises rates or signals more risesHigher US rates make dollars more rewarding to holdDollar strongerFewer
UK inflation, wages or growth stronger than forecastA Bank of England rise becomes more likelyPound strongerMore
The Bank of England raises rates or signals a riseHigher UK rates make pounds more rewarding to holdPound strongerMore
Oil jumps or tension in the Gulf risesInflation worries and safe-haven buying of dollarsDollar strongerFewer
Oil eases or tension calmsLess demand for dollars as a safe havenDollar weakerMore

These are the usual first reactions. A result the market already expected may move the rate very little, and two pieces of news on the same day can pull in opposite directions.

Prices come from Kraken's public GBP/USD, EUR/USD and EUR/GBP markets, which this page reads directly with no account. The live mid is halfway between Kraken's best bid and best offer. The chart history uses Kraken's own bars, which are traded prices and can sit a pip or two either side of the mid. ECB reference rates come from the Frankfurter service. The calendar comes from Forex Factory.

Dirhams are worked out at the UAE peg of 3.6725 to the dollar. On 25 September 2026 Kraken was checked against Yahoo Finance, Wise and the European Central Bank and agreed with them to within one to three GBP/USD pips. A dealer's own mid can differ by a pip or two.

The currency market shuts from 5pm New York on Friday to 5pm New York on Sunday, which is 01:00 Saturday to 01:00 Monday in Dubai while New York is on summer time. Kraken itself trades through the weekend, so the dashboard keeps showing its price as a guide to where the banks will reopen; weekend prices are left off the chart. All times are Dubai time unless marked London. Charts drawn with TradingView Lightweight Charts.
