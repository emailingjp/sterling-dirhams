Settings

Leave the rate blank to use the ECB reference rate for that day.

Your figures are kept in this browser on this device and are never sent anywhere. The page fetches only prices: Kraken's live GBP/USD market and the ECB reference rate for the day you choose.

Prices come from Kraken's public GBP/USD market, which this page reads directly with no account. The live mid is halfway between Kraken's best bid and best offer. The chart history uses Kraken's own bars, which are traded prices and can sit a pip or two either side of the mid. ECB reference rates come from the Frankfurter service.

Dirhams are worked out at the UAE peg of 3.6725 to the dollar. On 25 September 2026 Kraken was checked against Yahoo Finance, Wise and the European Central Bank and agreed with them to within one to three GBP/USD pips. A dealer's own mid can differ by a pip or two.

The currency market shuts from 5pm New York on Friday to 5pm New York on Sunday, which is 01:00 Saturday to 01:00 Monday in Dubai while New York is on summer time. Weekend prices are left off the chart. All times are Dubai time unless marked London. Charts drawn with TradingView Lightweight Charts.
