# Sterling into dirhams

A live GBP to AED tracker. It shows the mid rate, your rate after a dealing charge, what a sum of sterling brings in dirhams, and how that compares with a starting day.

Open it at https://emailingjp.github.io/sterling-dirhams/

Prices come from Kraken's public GBP/USD market and ECB reference rates from the Frankfurter service. Dirhams are worked out at the peg of 3.6725 to the dollar. The figures you enter stay in your own browser.
